# Dual-track submission strategy

## Deliverable model

Prepare two separately reviewable PDFs:

1. **Python Programming** — the approved historical report remains the
   known-good starting deliverable until an updated version is independently
   validated.
2. **Digital Marketing & Branding** — a future report covering Tasks 2–4 only
   after its evidence and QA gates are complete.

An optional combined dual-track portfolio may be generated only after the
organizer clarifies whether one Google Form response may contain both tracks.
The notice's phrase “ONE PDF” is ambiguous across multiple tracks.

## Privacy-safe identity insertion

Public repository documents and report templates use no participant name,
student ID, offer-letter image, email, phone, signature, or other identifying
data. If a submission requires identity, a user-controlled local copy may be
prepared from ignored private assets after explicit confirmation of the intended
track and recipient. No private insertion is automatic, committed, or needed
for technical review.

## Submission checks that remain open

- Confirm Python Programming track eligibility; the available offer-letter
  context concerns Digital Marketing only.
- Confirm whether the form accepts one or two track-specific submissions.
- Confirm permitted file count, file type, file size, and required naming.
- Confirm form access, recipient identity, registration details, and whether a
  completion certificate requires additional validation.
- Confirm the deadline hour and timezone for 18 October 2026.

No Google Form, LinkedIn post, offer-letter upload, or external publication may
occur until the user explicitly authorizes it.
