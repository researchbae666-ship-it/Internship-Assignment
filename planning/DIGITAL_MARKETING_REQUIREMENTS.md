## Shared evidence rules

- Record source URLs and UTC collection dates for public facts.
- Use one comparable observation window for all Task 2 competitors.
- Label values as **observed**, **estimated**, **assumed**, **target**, or
  **modeled**. Do not imply measured results with ambiguous prose.
- Cite formulas and units for engagement, budget, CAC, and ROAS.
- Preserve research notes, QA checks, and calculation inputs beside each task.
- Do not publish, spend, modify, or send anything externally.

## Task 3 execution record — 13 October 2026

The fictional RefillLoop campaign has a machine-readable 30-day calendar, a
validated USD 3,000 proposed media allocation, platform strategies, copy,
targeting assumptions, measurement plan, test plan, risk controls, and a
reviewable PDF at `digital_marketing/deliverables/DM_Task3_30_Day_Campaign.pdf`.
Its 53 planned actions span Instagram (30), LinkedIn (12), and YouTube (11).
Each action has a primary keyword, supporting terms, tags or video tags,
discovery intent, rationale, targeting identifier, and action-level copy. The
calendar maps all 15 paid actions to budget-pacing rows, which reconcile to
USD 1,350 for Instagram, USD 1,050 for LinkedIn, and USD 600 for YouTube. The
funnel explicitly models low, base, high, and stretch scenarios: the base case
is 120,000 impressions × 1.0% CTR × 2.5% form completion = 30 modeled interest
completions; 300 appears only as a stretch scenario requiring 240,000
impressions, 2.5% CTR, and 5.0% completion. All product, price, budget,
targeting, KPI, and performance values are labeled as assumptions or targets;
no campaign was published or funded.

## Task 4 execution record — 13 October 2026

The Ocean Bottle strategy records direct retrieval of four official public
pages, their observable titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, headings, and
internal route relationships. A B Lab directory URL returned HTTP 403 and is
explicitly excluded as independent validation. The strategy provides a
qualitative 12-keyword intent map, four-page on-page SEO map, four-stage
consent-aware acquisition funnel, four multi-step email workflows with ten full
message drafts, shared caps, priority, suppression and synthetic transition
tests. The formula-tested low/base/high model retains media CAC and ROAS and
adds all-in cost per customer using the GBP 12,000 project allocation. Two
reviewer-provided sources report differing 2019 and 2018 founding years; these
are attributed secondary observations with unavailable collection dates, not an
unqualified date claim. No ranking,
search-volume, analytics, campaign, revenue, or certification performance claim
is made without a verified source. All budgets, audiences, and financial values
are proposed model assumptions.
