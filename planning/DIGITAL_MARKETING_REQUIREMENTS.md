# Digital Marketing & Branding requirements

## Source and status

The available brief is `Digital Marketing & Branding Tasks & Instructions.md`.
It specifies Tasks 2–4 and broad page ranges but omits detailed grading
criteria, a sector, sources, and platform-account access. These omissions are
constraints, not gaps to fill with invented claims.

## Completion matrix

| Task | Required outcome | Minimum reviewable evidence | Completion criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2: competitor audit | 3–4-page audit of three consumer-sector firms; cadence, engagement estimates, visual/thematic position, market gaps | Source registry; comparable observation window; URL/date-backed post records; formulas; limitations; analysis draft | Every factual metric traces to a public observation or is unavailable; estimates are labeled; recommendations follow the analysis |
| 3: campaign roadmap | 30-day Instagram, LinkedIn, and YouTube campaign for an innovative B2B or consumer product | Product rationale, personas, positioning, target KPIs/assumptions, dated or relative-day matrix, copy, targeting, keyword/hashtag plan, measurement and risk plan | Calendar is executable and coherent across channels; paid allocations and results are labeled proposals/targets, never achieved facts |
| 4: SEO and acquisition strategy | 5–6-page strategy for a target startup website, including SEO, paid funnel, email re-engagement, budget/CAC/ROAS model | Public website observations; source-backed keyword discovery records; on-page map; funnel; email sequence; formulas, inputs, scenarios, limitations | Observed site facts are separate from recommendations; no private analytics access is claimed; financial results are transparent modeled projections |

## Shared evidence rules

- Record source URLs and UTC collection dates for public facts.
- Use one comparable observation window for all Task 2 competitors.
- Label values as **observed**, **estimated**, **assumed**, **target**, or
  **modeled**. Do not imply measured results with ambiguous prose.
- Cite formulas and units for engagement, budget, CAC, and ROAS.
- Preserve research notes, QA checks, and calculation inputs beside each task.
- Do not publish, spend, modify, or send anything externally.
