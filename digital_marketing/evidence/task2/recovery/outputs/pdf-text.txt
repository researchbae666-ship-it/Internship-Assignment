Calculation rules
With a verified full inventory: posts per week = observed period post count / 28 × 7. With a nonempty incomplete sample, the same arithmetic is labelled
observed minimum activity. With no verifiable sample, cadence is unavailable. Instagram proxy = (visible likes + visible comments) / exact visible followers ×
100; LinkedIn uses reactions + comments. YouTube views are not interchangeable with follower denominators. Every post requires official-account identity, URL,
publication and collection dates, evidence and metric precision.

Profile-display discrepancy
Rounded displays differed by response method: Stanley 1913 HTML 2M versus browser 1.7M; Hydro Flask HTML 630K versus browser 629K; Owala HTML 1M
versus browser 1.1M. These are verbatim retrieval snapshots, not precise follower estimates or growth measurements. Neither method's figure is substituted
into the unavailable period engagement calculation.
