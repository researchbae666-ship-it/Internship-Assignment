Assessment
The delivered pages expose canonical URLs, distinct titles, one visible H1 per sampled page, and links between product, FAQ, impact, collection, contact, and news routes. This supports a practical
topic-to-page model. Mobile experience, CWV, crawl coverage, structured-data validity, indexing, accessibility conformance, rankings, and analytics were not tested and remain implementation checks.

Conversion-path interpretation
Observed navigation and page relationships support a proposed path from impact or educational discovery to product review, then FAQ/delivery reassurance and add-to-cart. This is an interpretation of
public structure, not evidence of user behavior or conversion performance.

Automation decision table
Event / condition

Decision

Unsubscribe, consent withdrawal, bounce, complaint

Suppress immediately; clear active flow; no further marketing drafts.

Purchase / repeat purchase

Exit pre-purchase / post-purchase sequence; care entry needs confirmed delivery and consent.

Duplicate / priority

One flow at a time: post-purchase > cart > browse > welcome. Duplicate entries do not restart clocks.

Frequency cap / support issue

At most one message per 24h and three per rolling seven days; defer without advancing. Support pauses all messages.

Inactivity / deadline / completion

Expire or complete; no replay in pilot. Recheck consent and suppression before every message.

Media versus all-in economics
Media CAC retains the GBP 10,000 advertising denominator. Separately, all-in acquisition cost per customer = full GBP 12,000 project allocation / modeled customers: Low GBP 160.00, Base GBP 75.00,
High GBP 37.50. These are planning allocations, not accounting outcomes. ROAS always remains attributed revenue / advertising spend; creative costs do not change its denominator.

Recommended sequence
1. Validate claims, consent, tracking plan, and analytics governance. 2. Implement P1 product and impact maps. 3. Publish only approved, attributed content. 4. Instrument product, cart, purchase, and
email events. 5. Launch a small authorized test with a spend cap; compare outcomes against the model, not against assumed figures. 6. Reallocate only after a documented attribution and quality review.
