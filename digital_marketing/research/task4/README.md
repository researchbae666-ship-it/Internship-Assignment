# Task 4 research and calculation files

- `build_strategy.py` creates the machine-readable `strategy_data.json` and
  validates the budget, funnel, CAC, and ROAS arithmetic.
- `test_strategy.py` has standard-library coverage for strategy completeness,
  keyword intents, SEO map data, budget reconciliation, financial formulas,
  zero-denominator handling, funnel, email rules, and blocked-source handling.
- `SOURCE_REGISTRY.md` records the directly retrieved official sources and the
  inaccessible B Lab record.

All keyword opportunities are qualitative, intent-led proposals. No search
volume, difficulty, ranking, analytics, conversion, or ad-account data was
accessed or claimed.
