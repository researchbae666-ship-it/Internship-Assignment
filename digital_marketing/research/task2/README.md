# Task 2 research workspace

This directory holds the auditable record for the Multi-Platform Competitor
Brand Footprint Audit. The approved comparison sector is reusable hydration and
insulated drinkware; the research targets are Stanley 1913, Hydro Flask, and
Owala.

## Comparable observation window

The intended window is **15 September 2026 00:00:00 UTC through 12 October
2026 23:59:59 UTC**, inclusive (28 calendar days). This window applies to every
brand and platform. A post is included only when its official account, source
URL, and publication date can be independently observed.

At collection start, the cloud network proxy denied every requested official
brand domain and social-platform domain with HTTP status `000` and a CONNECT
403 error. Consequently, this revision contains no verified account URL, post,
metric, creative observation, or quantitative conclusion. It is a research
access record and audit framework, not a completed competitor audit.

See `SOURCE_REGISTRY.md`, `observations.json`, `metric_methodology.md`, and
`limitations.md`. The ignored raw command capture is
`artifacts/digital_marketing/task2/public_access_attempts.txt`.
